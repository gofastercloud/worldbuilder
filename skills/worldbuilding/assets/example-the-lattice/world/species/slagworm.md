## Description

A segmented burrower that grazes thermal mats and eats softened rock. It undermines warren foundations and mine shaft linings on Keth Prime and in the Dresh Holdings, so both the Keth'ri and the Dresh keep crews to seal its tunnels. Dresh tremor-sense picks it out of the stone easily.
