## Description

The backbone crop of Thuranni aquaculture. Long ribbons of kelp are grown in the Great Farms on Pelago and processed into the nutrient cultures that feed stations and ships across the Lattice. Small stands are kept in the Aquatic District on Haven and the Aquatic Quarter of Meridian Station for Thuranni residents and as ornamental water gardens.
