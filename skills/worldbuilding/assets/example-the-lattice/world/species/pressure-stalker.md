## Description

An ambush predator of the deep that feeds on lanternfish schools and is the main reason deep-ocean predators appear in the dangers listed for Pelago. Thuranni keep it away from the deep cities with sound barriers. Visitors in armoured submersibles are warned to treat it as deadly.
