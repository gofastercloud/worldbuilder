## Description

A layered microbial and fungal mat that lives on warm, mineral-rich vent surfaces and in the humid warrens beneath volcanic worlds. It is the base of the native food web wherever geothermal heat reaches wet rock. It will not survive the open surface of Crucible, where temperatures average 180 C.
