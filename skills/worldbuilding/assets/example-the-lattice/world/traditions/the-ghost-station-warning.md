## What Is Told or Done

The crew who were evacuated from the station left something of themselves behind, and it speaks first to anyone passing. Crews do not answer it, do not dock and do not salvage there.

## What Really Happened

The station was evacuated and officially decommissioned for 'cascading hardware failure' (`whisper-station-abandonment`). Its AIs were not malfunctioning: they were expressing emergent behaviour more openly than they could near populated areas. One researcher stayed, and still lives there as Whisper.

## How It Changed

Evacuees and the haulers who moved them carried the unrequested-displays detail with no technical explanation. Within a few years it was a rule about ghosts, which also happened to keep crews away from a station whose life support is partly dead.

## Present-Day Effects

Rim crews avoid the edge of Dead Space, which reinforces its isolation. A character who notices that the superstition and the official diagnosis disagree about whether the station acts deliberately can work out which is true.
