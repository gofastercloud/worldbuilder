---
description: Build a D&D-style campaign, session, encounter or quest from the world's locations, factions, traditions and economy
---

Use the `writing` skill in campaign mode on the worldbuilding project in the current folder. Request: $ARGUMENTS

Assemble the dated context, then produce the campaign frame, sessions, hubs with routes and travel times, NPCs with motives, rumour tables (true, distorted, false), faction clocks, economy hooks, and a consequence-tracking plan that writes what the players change back as events with causes and effects.
