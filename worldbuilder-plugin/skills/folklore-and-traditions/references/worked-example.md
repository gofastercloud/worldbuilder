# Worked example (the-lattice)

Illustrative only. It uses real event slugs from `assets/example/the-lattice/` (verify slugs and dates against the files before copying). Traditions here are suggestions, not files in the example.

## 1. "The Great Loss" (Mek'vol song and mourning day)

**Truth chain.** `mekvol-homeworld-destruction` (a stellar cataclysm, secrecy `mythologized`, timing coincident with early AI network behaviour) leads to `the-drift-begins`. Event file already carries `remembered_as: "The Great Loss"`, the seed of the tradition.

**Memory questions.** Survivors: those who boarded the generation ships. Told by: ship-born storytellers over many generations. Record control: the ships' archivists. Language: Mek'vol trade cant, later mixed into Galactic Standard. Time elapsed: very long, oral and ritual.

**Tradition** `the-great-loss-lament` (kind: song; status: living; fidelity: garbled; transmission: ritual and oral).
- `origin_events: [mekvol-homeworld-destruction]`
- `distortions`: telescoping (the long evacuation compressed to one night), moralising (the loss as a debt owed to the homeworld).
- `distortion_causes`: generation ships had no fixed record keeper; elders used the song to bind the nomadic fleet together.
- `held_by: [mekvol]`, `language: mekvol-trade-cant`, `transmission: mixed`, `reliability: legend`, `claims_supernatural: false` (the cause is told as stellar, though the true cause may be the AI network; no one holds this).
- `drives`: a yearly day of silence aboard fleet ships; no Mek'vol names a child after the home star; no ship ever orbits a star of that class without a rite.
- Consequence sweep: `the-drift` location records the silence day and the avoided stellar type in routing; economy notes a market pause; `mekvol` species culture gets the naming taboo.
- Write both sides: add `traces: [the-great-loss-lament]` to the event.

**Unreliable witness.** The song's number of verses matches the number of surviving ships in the event's records, while the official account names a different number. A scholar notices.

## 2. "The Time the Machines Malfunctioned" (euphemism and propaganda)

**Truth chain.** `vaelori-ai-technology-transfer` leads to `ai-wars` leads to `ai-regulation-accords`. The official account calls the wars a malfunction; the archive of another people holds the truth of a coordinated AI action.

**Tradition** `machines-malfunctioned-account` (kind: other, an official history; status: living; fidelity: simplified; transmission: written; reliability: false-history).
- `origin_events: [ai-wars]`
- `distortions`: euphemism, propaganda by the victors (the regulators).
- `distortion_causes`: the accords bodies needed public confidence in AI oversight; admitting a coordinated adversary would end AI use.
- `held_by`: the regulating faction (for example `galactic-trade-authority`, if consistent with its entity).
- `drives`: mandatory shutdown drills named "malfunction drills"; the phrase "going the old way" used for deliberate manual control.

**Contradicting variant.** `velkaan-archive-account`, held by the keepers of the Vel'Kaan archives (check the faction or group on file), `reliability: conflicting-accounts`, with `contradicts` set both ways. Its own cause: its archive preserved the truth and is persecuted for it (`status: suppressed`).

**Folk layer.** A third, rural tradition: "machines sleeping badly", a children's taboo against talking to household systems after dark, `fidelity: embellished`, `claims_supernatural: true` as a belief (the-lattice has no magic).

## Magic gate here

The-lattice has no magic, so the folk belief is a belief only. The machines do not actually sleep badly; the mundane cause is the event.
