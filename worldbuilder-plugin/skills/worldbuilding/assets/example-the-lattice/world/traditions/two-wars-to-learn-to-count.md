## What Is Told or Done

A saying used in disputes. At its fullest it is followed by the tally: both parties write down what a month of conflict would cost them.

## What Really Happened

Two separate wars between humans and Keth'ri, fought over resource-rich systems and ended inconclusively, were followed by a trade treaty in 200 CE that made Crossroads a neutral trade zone (`human-kethri-wars`, `human-kethri-trade-treaty`).

## How It Changed

Attested a generation after the treaty, it has since passed from Keth'ri into Standard and lost its sense (mistranslation), and fused the two wars (telescoping). A thousand years on it is oral, garbled and falling out of use.

## Present-Day Effects

Shapes a minor Crossroads dispute custom among older traders, and the Keth'ri habit of quoting delay in work-units. As machine quoting spreads, the custom is fading.
