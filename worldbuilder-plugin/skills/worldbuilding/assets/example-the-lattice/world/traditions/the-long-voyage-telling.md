## What Is Told or Done

The Ground (the lost homeworld) held its children as long as it could. When it knew they were ready, it let go, so that they would carry what they knew to every shore that had none. The Drift is therefore not exile but a task. The telling is recited standing, in Trade Cant, with a fixed opening formula in older syllables that the reciters no longer understand.

## What Really Happened

A stellar event, officially, made the Mek'vol home system uninhabitable and the survivors fled on generation ships (`mekvol-homeworld-destruction`, `the-drift-begins`). The real cause is undocumented. There was no choice and no gift.

## How It Changed

Over about two millennia the grief of a ruined people was recast as a purpose (moralising). The original language died with the homeworld and the formula was reinterpreted in Trade Cant (mistranslation), which turned a possibly passive loss into an act of will. The Reclaimers' legend `the-ground-was-taken` is the rival reading.

## Present-Day Effects

Shapes the Great Gatherings, the traditionalist refusal to settle, and the sanctity of shared information inside a ship-clan. Opposition to it is the cultural fault line the Reclaimers sit on.
