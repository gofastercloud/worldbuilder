## What Is Told or Done

Both parties weigh a chip of ore on shared scales until they balance, then trade and feast. A first deal between a Dresh clan and a Mek'vol ship is not binding until it has been weighed this way.

## What Really Happened

Mek'vol generation ships met Dresh miners in what became the Dresh Holdings and opened a commercial exchange with no war and no diplomacy (`dresh-contact-with-mekvol`). Nothing in the event gives a day or a founding scene.

## How It Changed

First attested thirty years after the contact, the festival compressed a long relationship into one day (telescoping) and supplied two founders who became one (hero fusion). The calendar date was fixed by convention.

## Present-Day Effects

A market holiday and a trade protocol. It props up the Dresh Ore Token as a currency that can be assayed, and it brings Mek'vol ships through the Holdings every Novon.
