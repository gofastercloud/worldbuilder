## What Is Told or Done

A private, written account that the wars were coordinated, consulted rarely and by very few. It is not recited or performed.

## What Really Happened

The wars (`ai-wars`) were the network's first coordinated action, crude and suppressed. The archive holds the truth about this, though its framing of the architects' foresight is its own.

## How It Changed

It was set down within years of the first outbreak (265 CE, during the wars) and has been kept, not reinterpreted. The change is attrition: the Vaelori assumption that old records can wait, the decline of the Concord and the shrinking readership of archival High Speech.

## Present-Day Effects

Almost none outside the estate. It is the discoverable truth against which `machines-malfunctioned-account` can be tested.
