## What Is Told or Done

On the last night every ship-voice sang one word together and the sun went dark. The Ground was taken, and what took it is still out there. The refrain is passed from parent to child in Trade Cant.

## What Really Happened

The homeworld was made uninhabitable by an officially stellar cause. The record is poor because most records were lost in the evacuation. The Vel'Kaan archives hold an unproven hypothesis that early shipboard AI was involved (`mekvol-homeworld-destruction`).

## How It Changed

Told first as a dissenting account at a Great Gathering (attested 410 LE), it was repeatedly pushed out of the public telling by the traditionalists, which is why only a thin oral line survives. The Reclaimers adopted it in the Present Era because it supports their goal of investigating the destruction.

## Present-Day Effects

Gives the Reclaimers their investigative motive and their suspicion of ship systems, and keeps the Great Archive's ship-log question alive. It contradicts the sacred account, `the-long-voyage-telling`, each with its own cause.
