## What Is Told or Done

Shift crews sing it as they count each load: an even count against an uneven payment, closing on the clerk and the Signer. It is a work-song first and a protest song second.

## What Really Happened

Corporate-backed contracts from 200 LE bound rim settlements, notably Dresh mines, to below-market extraction. The agreements were drafted with AI advisory assistance and are technically legal (`rim-exploitation-contracts`). Resentment then accumulated into open Frontier Unrest from 1 PE (`frontier-unrest-begins`).

## How It Changed

Attested within a generation of the first contracts, it has been rewritten by every later grievance (conflation). After Frontier Unrest began, the GTA's rim patrols began treating it as incitement and it was pushed to private performance and The Bazaar. The mistranslation into Standard moved its one accurate clue from machine to metaphor.

## Present-Day Effects

A banned chorus is now a marker of allegiance. It gives the League and the Guilds shared ground, and it plants the clue that the contracts' drafter did not sleep.
