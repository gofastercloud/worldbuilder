# Sizes

The user's size sets scope. Counts are guides, not quotas: stop when the world is coherent and every entity is explained, even if under the range; exceed a range only if the causal chains demand it.

**Events and eras are not set here.** Use the pacing table in `skills/history-and-events/SKILL.md` ("How many events") for total events, significant events, per-era counts and number of eras. Do not copy or restate those numbers elsewhere.

## Entity guide (excluding events)

| Entity | S | M | L | XL |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| species (sentient) | 1 to 3 | 2 to 5 | 4 to 10 | 8 to 20 |
| races (sub-groups) | 1 to 4 | 3 to 10 | 6 to 20 | 12 to 40 |
| languages | 1 to 3 | 2 to 6 | 4 to 12 | 8 to 25 |
| locations | 2 to 6 | 5 to 15 | 12 to 35 | 25 to 80 |
| factions | 1 to 3 | 2 to 6 | 4 to 12 | 8 to 25 |
| characters | 3 to 8 | 8 to 20 | 20 to 50 | 40 to 100 |
| items | 0 to 3 | 2 to 8 | 5 to 15 | 10 to 30 |
| lineages | 0 to 2 | 1 to 4 | 3 to 8 | 5 to 15 |
| traditions (each derived from an event) | 2 to 4 | 5 to 10 | 10 to 20 | 20 to 40 |
| arcs | 1 to 2 | 2 to 4 | 3 to 8 | 5 to 15 |
| magic systems (only if magic is present) | 1 | 1 to 2 | 1 to 3 | 2 to 5 |

## Other scope guides

| | S | M | L | XL |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| History depth (years) | 50 to 500 | 200 to 2,000 | 1,000 to 10,000 | 5,000 to 50,000 |
| Geography depth (levels) | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 to 7 |
| Transport modes | 1 to 3 | 2 to 5 | 3 to 8 | 4 to 12 |
| Currencies | 1 | 1 to 3 | 2 to 5 | 3 to 10 |
| Resources | 2 to 5 | 4 to 10 | 8 to 20 | 12 to 40 |
| Trade routes | 0 to 2 | 1 to 5 | 3 to 10 | 5 to 25 |

History depth should also match lifespans and the technology level: do not give a medieval-tech world 40,000 years of recorded history without saying what was lost.

## Cautions

- Do not pick XL for a short story. If unsure, start at M and extend later with the `worldbuilding` skill.
- At L and XL, run stages in several passes (a region or a people at a time) and validate between passes.
- Under magic `present: false`, the magic-systems row is 0.
